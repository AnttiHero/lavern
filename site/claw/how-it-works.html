The Idea

Not One Agent. A Firm.

Most AI legal tools are a single prompt wrapped in a UI. Lavern is modeled on how a real law firm works: 58 specialist agents and 7 orchestrators who coordinate through structured debate.

The contract reviewer finds a risk. The adversarial attacker challenges it. The synthesizer weighs the evidence. The orchestrator resolves the debate. Just like partners arguing in a conference room — except it takes 90 seconds instead of 3 billable hours.

The Core Mechanism

Debate Is a Feature.

Agents don't just analyze — they argue. The Debate Board is shared state where agents post findings, challenge each other, respond with evidence, and resolve disputes. This adversarial structure catches issues that a single pass would miss.

Every resolution is a first-class auditable event. An insurance reviewer can ask: "Why did the system flag this clause?" and trace the answer through findings, challenges, evidence, and the winning position.

Institutional Memory

The Firm Gets Smarter.

Every document Clawern reviews makes it smarter. Findings are indexed into a Precedent Board — persistent memory. Before processing future documents, matching precedents are injected as context.

Key insight: A liability cap pattern found in an NDA in January informs the review of a different NDA in March — automatically, with no human intervention. The firm compounds its knowledge.

Principles

What We Believe.

1. Legal effect must remain identical after transformation.
Every word matters. Redesigned documents look different but mean the same thing.
2. Every finding must cite specific text as evidence.
No vague warnings. The exact clause, every time.
3. Debate is a feature, not a bug.
Disagreement produces better outcomes than consensus.
4. Human gates are mandatory.
The system assists, never decides. Critical moments pause for human judgment.
5. This is not legal advice.
Always verify with qualified legal professionals.
