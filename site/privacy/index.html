Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 May 2026

We don't collect anything from you on this site. No cookies. No accounts. No forms. No tracking across sessions or across sites. No data sold, shared, or sublicensed.

If you email us, we keep the correspondence in our inbox until the thread is closed. That is the entirety of the personal data processing this site performs.

What loads from third parties

Plausible Analytics (plausible.io) is loaded on every page to count anonymous page views. Plausible is an EU-based service that uses no cookies, no localStorage, no personal data, no fingerprinting, and no cross-site tracking. Their public data policy: plausible.io/data-policy.

Netlify hosts the static files. Standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) may be retained briefly by Netlify for security and abuse purposes, governed by Netlify's own privacy policy.

That is the complete list. No other script, pixel, beacon, or external resource is loaded.
